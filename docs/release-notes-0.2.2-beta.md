Lacquer 0.2.2 beta

A maintenance build. Nothing about how discs are made has changed since 0.2.1.

- The sample jobs that `--snapshot` renders have new names, and the queue screenshots in `docs/screenshots` are re-rendered from them.
- This build replaces 0.2.0 and 0.2.1, which are no longer offered.
- No Intel build.

Files
- Lacquer-0.2.2-arm64.dmg: the app, signed and notarized.
- Lacquer-0.2.2-source.tar.gz: corresponding source for the app and the bundled FFmpeg, x264, zimg and tsMuxeR, with the build scripts.
- SHA256SUMS
